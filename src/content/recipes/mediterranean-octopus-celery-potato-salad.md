1. साफ किए हुए ऑक्टोपस को बिना नमक वाले पानी में धीमी आँच पर 45 से 50 मिनट तक नरम होने तक पकाएँ। उसे शोरबे में थोड़ा ठंडा होने दें, फिर भुजाओं को 1 सेंटीमीटर के गोल टुकड़ों में काटें।

# Mediterranean Octopus, Celery, and Warm Potato Salad

High-protein coastal Italian-style salad combining tender simmered octopus slices with warm waxy potatoes, crisp celery, parsley, and lemon-olive oil dressing.

## Instructions
1. Simmer the cleaned octopus gently in unsalted water for 45 to 50 minutes until knife-tender, let cool slightly in the broth, then slice the tentacles into 1 cm rounds.
2. Boil the halved waxy potatoes in a separate pot for 14 minutes until tender, then drain and steam-dry for 2 minutes.
3. Whisk the extra-virgin olive oil, fresh lemon juice, and minced garlic in a large mixing bowl.
4. Toss the warm potatoes, sliced octopus, crisp celery, red onion, and fresh parsley in the dressing and let marinate 10 minutes before serving.
:::
