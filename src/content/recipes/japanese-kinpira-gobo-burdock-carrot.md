instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Soak the burdock matchsticks in cold water for 5 minutes to prevent
      browning and remove harshness, then drain and pat thoroughly dry.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Heat the toasted sesame oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat and
      stir-fry the burdock root and chili flakes for 3 minutes.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add the carrot matchsticks and stir-fry 2 minutes more until glossy and
      slightly tender.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Pour in the kombu dashi, low-sodium tamari, rice vinegar, and maple syrup;
      cook uncovered over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, tossing often until
      the glaze evaporates and coats the roots.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Remove from heat, toss with toasted white sesame seeds, and serve warm or
      at room temperature.
