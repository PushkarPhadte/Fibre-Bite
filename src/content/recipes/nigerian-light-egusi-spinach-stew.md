instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Blend the red bell peppers, tomatoes, half the onion, garlic, and ginger
      into a smooth red puree; dice the remaining onion finely.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Mix the ground egusi seeds with 90 ml of water and a pinch of salt to form
      a thick, scoopable paste.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Heat the sunflower oil in a Dutch oven, sauté the diced onion for 3
      minutes, pour in the pepper-tomato puree and spices, and simmer uncovered
      for 12 minutes until reduced.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Add the vegetable broth and remaining salt, then drop tablespoonfuls of
      the egusi paste onto the simmering surface; cover without stirring for 10
      minutes so tender curds set.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Gently break the curds into bite-sized pieces, fold in the chopped
      spinach, and simmer 3 minutes until bright green and wilted.
