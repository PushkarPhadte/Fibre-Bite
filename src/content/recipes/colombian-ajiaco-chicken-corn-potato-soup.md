instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Place the chicken breast, low-sodium chicken broth, 250 ml of water,
      sliced scallions, smashed garlic, half the cilantro-guascas herbs, and
      salt into a Dutch oven and bring to a gentle simmer.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Poach the chicken gently for 15 minutes until cooked through (74°C
      internally), transfer to a plate to shred with two forks, and cover to
      keep warm.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add the small-diced potatoes and corn to the simmering broth and cook 15
      minutes until the small potatoes break down to naturally thicken the soup.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Add the larger cubed potatoes and simmer 12 minutes more until tender yet
      intact, then return the shredded chicken to warm through.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Ladle into warm bowls and garnish with fresh cilantro, sliced avocado, and
      rinsed capers.
