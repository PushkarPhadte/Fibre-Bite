instructions:

      or sear on a hot cast-iron griddle for 4 minutes per side until lightly
      charred and tender (grilling minimizes mucilage).
  - number: 2
    text: Cool the grilled nopales slightly and dice them into neat 1 cm squares.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Whisk the remaining 10 ml of olive oil, fresh lime juice, dried Mexican
      oregano, and salt in a large salad bowl.
  - number: 4
    text: >-

      cilantro with the lime dressing, then gently fold in the diced avocado and
