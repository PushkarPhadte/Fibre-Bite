instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Knead the besan with the 30 g curd, cumin, 1 g chilli, 1 g turmeric, 1.5 g
      salt, 5 ml of the oil and a splash of water into a firm dough.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Roll into 4 cm thick ropes and boil in 500 ml of simmering water for 12
      minutes until they float and feel firm. Lift out, reserve the water, cool
      and cut into 2 cm pieces.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Whisk the 200 g curd with the remaining turmeric, the coriander powder,
      chilli and 2 g of salt until smooth.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Heat the remaining oil, fry the ginger and garlic for 1 minute, add the
      tomato puree and cook for 4 minutes. Lower the heat, stir in the curd
      mixture and cook for 5 minutes, stirring constantly so it does not split.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Add the dumplings and 150 ml of the reserved water and simmer for 8
      minutes. Garnish with coriander.
