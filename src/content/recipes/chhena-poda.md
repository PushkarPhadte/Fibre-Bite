slug: chhena-poda
description: >-
  A healthy version of Odisha's famous baked cottage cheese dessert made with
  low-fat paneer, jaggery, and cardamom.
baseServings: 4
ingredients:
  - name: 'fresh low-fat paneer (chhena), crumbled'
    baseAmount: 200
    unit: g
  - name: organic jaggery powder
    baseAmount: 3
    unit: tbsp
  - name: semolina (sooji)
    baseAmount: 1
    unit: tbsp
  - name: cardamom powder
    baseAmount: 0.5
    unit: tsp
  - name: crushed cashew nuts and raisins
    baseAmount: 1
    unit: tbsp
  - name: 'ghee, for greasing'
    baseAmount: 1
    unit: tsp
instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Knead crumbled chhena with jaggery powder, semolina, and cardamom powder
      until soft.
  - number: 2
    text: Mix in crushed cashews and raisins.
  - number: 3
    text: Transfer mixture into a ghee-greased baking dish or cake tin.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Bake at 180°C for 30–35 minutes until the top turns golden brown and
      caramelized.
