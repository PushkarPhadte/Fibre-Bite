## Ingredients

- 500 g chicken breast
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp parsley
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken with lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, and herbs.
2. Marinate for 15 minutes while the oven preheats to 220°C.
3. Roast or pan-sear until the chicken is cooked through and golden.
4. Finish with extra lemon zest and serve immediately.
