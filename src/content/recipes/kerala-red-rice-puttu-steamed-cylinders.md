instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Mix the red rice flour, cardamom, and salt in a wide bowl, then sprinkle
      warm water gradually while rubbing with your fingertips until the flour
      resembles damp breadcrumbs that hold together lightly when pressed.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Rest the hydrated flour covered for 10 minutes so the coarse whole-grain
      particles absorb moisture evenly.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Fill a puttu kutti steamer cylinder in alternating layers of freshly
      grated coconut and damp red rice flour, finishing with coconut on top
      without packing tightly.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Fit the cylinder onto a boiling puttu steamer base and steam for 8 to 10
      minutes until steady steam rises freely from the top vents; push the
      cylinder gently onto a plate and serve hot.
