instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Drain the soaked yellow peas and pressure-cook or simmer in 750 ml of
      water with 1 g of salt until soft yet intact, keeping the starchy cooking
      broth.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Heat the mustard oil in a wide pot over medium heat and sauté
      three-quarters of the chopped onion, ginger, and garlic for 5 minutes
      until golden.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add the diced tomatoes and two-thirds of the bhaja masala, cooking 5
      minutes until the tomatoes break down into a rich base.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Pour in the cooked yellow peas with their broth and remaining salt,
      simmering uncovered for 10 minutes until lightly thickened.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Finish with the remaining roasted bhaja masala, fresh lime juice, raw
      diced onion, and chopped cilantro.
