instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Toss the fish chunks with half the lime juice, half the minced garlic, and
      1 g of salt; let marinate 15 minutes.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Warm the olive oil in a wide clay pot or Dutch oven over medium heat,
      sauté the onion rings, red and yellow bell peppers, and remaining garlic
      for 6 minutes until softened.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Stir in the sweet paprika, cumin, diced tomatoes, light coconut milk, and
      remaining salt, simmering gently for 6 minutes.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Nestle the marinated fish pieces into the simmering pepper-coconut broth,
      cover, and poach on low heat for 6 to 8 minutes until opaque and flaky.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Finish with the remaining fresh lime juice and a generous scattering of
      chopped cilantro.
