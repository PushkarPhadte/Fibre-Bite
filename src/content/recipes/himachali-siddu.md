  - name: active dry yeast

  - name: 'walnuts, coarsely crushed'

    unit: g
  - name: 'green chillies, minced'

    unit: whole
  - name: 'ginger, grated'

  - name: salt

    unit: to taste
  - name: spices
    baseAmount: 0

  - name: 'water, warm, for the dough'
    baseAmount: 0
    unit: as needed
instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Knead wheat flour with warm water, yeast, and salt into a soft dough;
      leave to ferment for 2 hours.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Prepare stuffing by mixing ground poppy seeds, walnuts, chillies, ginger,
      and salt.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Flatten fermented dough portions, fill with poppy seed mixture, fold into
      half-moon shapes, and seal edges.
  - number: 4
    text: Steam in a steamer for 15–20 minutes and serve warm with dal or chutney.
