instructions:

    text: >-
      Whisk the flour, water and a pinch of the salt into a thin batter and rest
      for 10 minutes.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Heat 1 teaspoon of the oil in a pan and cook the cumin and onion until
      soft; add the spinach and cook for 2 minutes, then stir in the paneer and
      the remaining salt.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Cook the batter in a lightly oiled non-stick pan as four thin crepes,
      about 2 minutes per side.
  - number: 4
    text: Fill each crepe with the paneer mixture and fold.
