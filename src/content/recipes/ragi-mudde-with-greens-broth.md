instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Simmer the toor dal and chopped amaranth greens in 750 ml of water with
      1.5 g of salt for 20 minutes until tender; strain the flavorful cooking
      broth and blend one-quarter of the cooked dal with the tomato and
      cumin-pepper blend.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Heat the sunflower oil in a saucepan, crackle the mustard seeds, curry
      leaves, and crushed garlic, then pour in the strained greens broth and
      pureed dal mixture and simmer 5 minutes.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      For the ragi mudde, whisk 20 g of ragi flour into 450 ml of water with the
      remaining 1 g of salt in a heavy pot and bring to a rolling boil.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Sprinkle the remaining 180 g of ragi flour over the surface without
      stirring, cover and steam on low heat for 4 minutes, then vigorously stir
      with a sturdy wooden handle until smooth and glossy.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Moisten your hands with cold water, shape the warm dough into four smooth
      balls, and serve immediately with the hot greens broth.
