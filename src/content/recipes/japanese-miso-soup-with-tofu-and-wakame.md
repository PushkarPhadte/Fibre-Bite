instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: Soak dried wakame seaweed in warm water for 5 minutes to rehydrate.
  - number: 2
    text: Heat kombu dashi broth in a saucepan until hot (do not boil).
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Ladle out a little warm broth into a cup, add miso paste, and whisk until
      smooth.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Add tofu and wakame to pot, stir in dissolved miso paste, turn off heat,
      and serve topped with spring onions.
