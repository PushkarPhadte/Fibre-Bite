instructions:

      water, turmeric, tomatoes, and salt.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Bring to a boil, skim any foam, then simmer partly covered until the
      lentils are soft, about 20 minutes. Add water if needed.
  - number: 3
    text: >-

  - number: 4
    text: >-
