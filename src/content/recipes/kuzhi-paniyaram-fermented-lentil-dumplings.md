instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Heat 6 ml of sunflower oil in a small pan, splutter the mustard seeds and
      chopped curry leaves, sauté the onion and green chili for 2 minutes, and
      cool slightly.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Fold the tempered aromatics, grated carrot, chopped cilantro, and salt
      into the fermented brown rice and urad dal batter until evenly
      distributed.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Preheat a cast-iron paniyaram (aebleskiver) pan over medium-low heat and
      brush each cavity lightly with the remaining 10 ml of sunflower oil.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Fill each cavity three-quarters full with batter, cover for 3 minutes
      until the undersides turn golden brown, then flip with a wooden skewer and
      cook 2 to 3 minutes more until crisp outside and airy inside.
