  Lightened Rajasthani classic featuring steamed spiced chickpea flour
  medallions simmered in a tangy, aromatic low-fat yogurt and tomato gravy
  instead of deep-frying.

instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Knead the chickpea flour with 40 g of yogurt, 4 ml of mustard oil, half
      the spice blend, 1 g of salt, and 30 ml of water into a firm dough; roll
      into 1.5 cm thick logs.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Steam or gently poach the dough logs for 12 minutes until firm and cooked
      through, cool slightly, and slice into 1 cm medallions (reserving 150 ml
      of poaching water if poached).
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Heat the remaining 10 ml of mustard oil in a saucepan, sauté the onion and
      ginger-garlic for 4 minutes, add the tomato puree and remaining spices,
      and cook 5 minutes.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Lower the heat, whisk in the remaining 240 g of yogurt and 150 ml of warm
      water while stirring continuously until gently simmering.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Add the sliced gatte and remaining salt, simmer covered for 8 minutes
      until tender and saucy, and garnish with fresh cilantro.
