  - name: sesame seeds

  - name: green chilli-ginger paste

  - name: turmeric powder

  - name: 'oil, for pan-sautéing'

  - name: salt

instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Mix chopped coriander, besan, rice flour, green chilli-ginger paste,
      sesame seeds, turmeric, and salt into a thick dough.
  - number: 2
    text: Shape into a cylindrical roll and steam in a steamer for 15 minutes.
  - number: 3
    text: Allow to cool completely and slice into 1/2-inch thick cakes.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Pan-sauté sliced vadis in a tablespoon of oil until outer surfaces are
      crisp.
