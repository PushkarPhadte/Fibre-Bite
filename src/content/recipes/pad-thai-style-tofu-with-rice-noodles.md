# Pad Thai Style Tofu with Rice Noodles

A lighter pad Thai with tofu, bean sprouts, peanuts, tamarind and lime.

## Instructions
1. Soak the noodles in hot water until just tender and drain.
2. Brown the tofu in half the oil for 5 minutes and remove.
3. Stir-fry the garlic and carrot in the remaining oil, add the noodles, tamarind, tamari, lime juice and chilli and toss.
4. Return the tofu, add the sprouts and spring onions for 1 minute and top with peanuts.
:::
