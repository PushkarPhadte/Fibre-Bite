instructions:

    text: >-
      Warm 10 ml of olive oil in a heavy saucepan, sauté the chopped yellow
      onion and garlic for 4 minutes until translucent, then stir in the rinsed
      yellow split peas.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Pour in 750 ml of water, bring to a boil while skimming any surface foam,
      then reduce to low heat and simmer partially covered for 35 to 40 minutes
      until the peas break down completely.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Stir in the salt, lemon juice, and 8 ml of olive oil, then puree with an
      immersion blender until satin-smooth.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Spread onto a shallow serving dish and top with the diced red onion,
      rinsed capers, remaining 10 ml of olive oil, Greek oregano, and chopped
      parsley.
