instructions:

      Heat the sunflower oil in a soup pot over medium heat and sauté the minced
      shallots and sliced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Pour in 900 ml of water, the strained tamarind liquid, and reduced-sodium
      fish sauce, bringing the broth to a lively simmer.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add the pineapple wedges and tomato wedges and simmer for 4 minutes so
      their natural sweetness infuses the tangy broth.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Gently lower in the white fish chunks and sliced okra, poaching on
      medium-low heat for 5 minutes until the fish turns opaque and okra is
      tender-crisp.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Turn off the heat, stir in the crisp mung bean sprouts, and top generously
      with chopped cilantro and scallions.
