instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Blanch spinach in boiling water for 1 minute, transfer to cold water,
      drain, and blend to a smooth puree.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Warm half the oil in a pan and lightly brown the paneer on two sides.
      Remove and set aside.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add the remaining oil. Cook onion until soft, then add ginger, garlic,
      cumin, and tomato; cook until the tomato softens.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Stir in the spinach puree and garam masala and simmer gently for 5
      minutes. Lower the heat, stir in yogurt, then fold in paneer and warm
      without boiling. Season and serve.
