:::lang en
# Foxtail Millet Tomato Cucumber Salad

A cold salad of cooked foxtail millet with cucumber, tomato, coriander and lemon.

## Instructions
1. Cook the millet in 250 ml of water for 15 minutes, fluff and cool completely.
2. Toss with the cucumber, tomato, onion, coriander, lemon juice, oil and salt.
3. Top with the peanuts.
:::
