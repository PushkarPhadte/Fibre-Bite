instructions:

      for 30 seconds.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Add the sliced onion and julienned ginger and sweat gently for 4 minutes
      until translucent without browning.
  - number: 3
    text: >-

      water; cover and simmer for 12 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  - number: 4
    text: >-

      for 4 minutes without boiling vigorously, then finish with the remaining
      fresh curry leaves.
