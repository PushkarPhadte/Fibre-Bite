instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Rinse the poha briefly in a sieve, drain well, and leave it to soften for
      8 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Heat the oil in a wide pan. Add mustard and cumin seeds; when they
      crackle, stir in the peanuts and cook until lightly toasted.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add the onion, potato, peas, chilli, turmeric, and salt. Cover and cook
      over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until the potato is tender.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Fold in the softened poha and warm gently for 3 to 4 minutes. Turn off the
      heat, add lemon juice and cilantro, and serve warm.
