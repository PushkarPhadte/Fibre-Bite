instructions:

    text: >-

      drain thoroughly, and pass through a ricer or mash until completely smooth
      while warm.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Fold 35 ml of lime juice, the olive oil, aji amarillo-turmeric seasoning,
      and 1 g of salt into the warm potato mash and cool to room temperature.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Combine the flaked tuna, plain yogurt, remaining 15 ml of lime juice,
      minced red onion, diced celery, cilantro, and remaining salt in a bowl.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Press half the seasoned potato mixture into a lined 18 cm dish or ring
      molds, layer with sliced avocado and the tuna salad, cap with the
      remaining potato mash, and chill 30 minutes before slicing.
