instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Drain the soaked chana dal and toor dal and pulse in a food processor with
      the yogurt and 60 ml of water into a coarse, thick batter.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Stir in the sorghum flour, grated bottle gourd, grated carrot, ginger,
      turmeric-cumin powder, and salt; rest covered for 30 minutes while
      preheating the oven to 190°C.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Heat the sunflower oil in an ovenproof 22 cm cast-iron skillet, crackle
      the mustard seeds, curry leaves, and half the sesame seeds, then turn off
      the heat.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Fold the baking soda into the batter, spread evenly into the tempered
      skillet, and scatter the remaining sesame seeds over the surface.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Bake for 32 to 35 minutes until golden brown and crisp at the edges; rest
      10 minutes before slicing into wedges.
