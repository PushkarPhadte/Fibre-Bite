instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Roughly chop the washed mustard greens and spinach, place them in a heavy
      pot with half the ginger, half the garlic, green chili, salt, and 180 ml
      of water.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Simmer covered over medium-low heat for 25 minutes until completely
      tender, then mash coarsely with a wooden masher or pulse briefly.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Sprinkle the maize flour gradually into the greens while stirring
      continuously, and simmer on low heat for 10 minutes until velvety.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Heat the mustard oil in a separate skillet until lightly smoking, cool
      briefly, and sauté the remaining ginger, garlic, and diced onion for 4
      minutes.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Add the chopped tomato and cook until jammy, then fold the tempering into
      the saag and simmer 3 minutes before serving.
