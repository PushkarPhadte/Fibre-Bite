instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Whisk the ragi flour, rice flour, salt, and water until smooth. The batter
      should be thin and pourable; rest it for 15 minutes.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Stir in onion, cumin, chilli, and cilantro. Mix again before each dosa
      because the flour settles.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and wipe with a little oil.
      Pour a ladle of batter from the outside toward the center, leaving small
      holes for crisping.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Drizzle a little oil around the edge and cook until crisp and browned
      underneath. Fold and serve hot; repeat with the remaining batter.
