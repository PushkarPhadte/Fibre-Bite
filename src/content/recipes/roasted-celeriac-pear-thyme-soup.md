# Roasted Celeriac and Pear Soup with Thyme

Naturally creamy, dairy-free autumn velouté blending oven-caramelized celery root and sweet ripe pear with sautéed leeks and fragrant fresh thyme.

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C, toss the cubed celeriac and pear quarters with 12 ml of olive oil and half the thyme, and roast for 30 minutes until tender and lightly browned.
2. Warm the remaining 8 ml of olive oil in a soup pot over medium-low heat and sweat the sliced leeks and smashed garlic for 6 minutes until soft.
3. Add the roasted celeriac, pears, remaining thyme leaves, vegetable broth, and salt; simmer gently for 10 minutes.
4. Puree the soup with an immersion blender until silky smooth, stir in the fresh lemon juice to brighten, and serve hot.
:::
