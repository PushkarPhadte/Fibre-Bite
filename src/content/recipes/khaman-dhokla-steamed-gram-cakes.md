instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Whisk the sifted chickpea flour, 200 ml of water, 20 ml of lemon juice,
      grated ginger, minced green chili, turmeric, and salt into a smooth,
      lump-free batter and rest for 10 minutes.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Bring water to a boil in a steamer and lightly brush an 18 cm round
      heatproof pan with 2 ml of sunflower oil.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Fold the baking soda gently into the batter until light and frothy,
      immediately pour into the prepared pan, and steam covered over medium-high
      heat for 15 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Heat the remaining 8 ml of sunflower oil in a small pan, crackle the
      mustard seeds and curry leaves for 30 seconds, then stir in the remaining
      40 ml of water and 10 ml of lemon juice.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Cool the steamed dhokla for 5 minutes, slice into squares, spoon the warm
      tempering evenly over the top, and garnish with chopped cilantro.
