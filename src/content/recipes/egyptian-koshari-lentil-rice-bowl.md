instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Toss the thinly sliced onions with 8 ml of olive oil and roast at 210°C
      for 22 minutes, stirring halfway, until deeply golden and crisp.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Simmer the brown rice in 250 ml of water for 15 minutes, then add the
      rinsed brown lentils, 300 ml more water, 1 g of salt, and 2 g of cumin;
      cover and cook 20 minutes more until tender and fluffy.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Meanwhile, boil the whole-wheat macaroni until al dente and drain; warm
      the cooked chickpeas gently.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Heat the remaining 10 ml of olive oil in a saucepan, sauté the minced
      garlic and remaining spices for 1 minute, add the vinegar and pureed
      tomatoes with remaining salt, and simmer 12 minutes.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Layer the lentil-rice base, whole-wheat macaroni, spiced tomato sauce,
      warm chickpeas, and crispy roasted onions in bowls.
