instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Soak chickpeas overnight, drain, and pressure-cook in fresh water until
      tender. Reserve some cooking liquid.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Heat oil in a pan and cook the onion until golden. Add ginger and garlic
      and stir for 1 minute.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add cumin, coriander, and chana masala powder. Stir briefly, then add
      tomatoes and cook until they break down into a thick masala.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Add chickpeas, amchur, salt, and enough reserved liquid to make a thick
      curry. Simmer for 15 minutes, lightly mashing a few chickpeas to thicken.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Finish with lemon juice and cilantro. Serve hot with rice or whole-grain
      flatbread.
