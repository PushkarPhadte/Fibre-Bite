# Paneer Bhurji Whole Wheat Roll

Spiced crumbled paneer with onion, tomato and pepper rolled in whole wheat chapati.

## Instructions
1. Knead the flour with water into a dough and roll into 3 thin chapatis; cook on a dry griddle.
2. Heat the oil, splutter the cumin and cook the onion and pepper for 4 minutes.
3. Add the tomato and spices, cook for 3 minutes, then add the paneer and salt and cook for 3 minutes.
4. Spoon into the chapatis, add coriander and roll up.
:::
