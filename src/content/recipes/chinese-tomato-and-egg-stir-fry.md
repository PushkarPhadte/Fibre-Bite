instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Beat the eggs with 0.5 g of salt and 15 ml of water so they stay extra
      fluffy and tender.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Heat 8 ml of sunflower oil in a wok or nonstick skillet over medium-high
      heat, pour in the eggs, push gently into soft curds for 45 seconds until
      80% set, and immediately slide onto a plate.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Add the remaining 4 ml of sunflower oil, sauté the scallion whites, sliced
      garlic, and ginger for 30 seconds, then stir-fry the tomato wedges and
      remaining salt for 4 minutes until juicy and saucy.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Return the soft eggs to the wok, fold gently into the tomato sauce for 30
      seconds, drizzle with toasted sesame oil, and scatter with scallion
      greens.
