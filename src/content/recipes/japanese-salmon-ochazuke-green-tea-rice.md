instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: Cook the brown rice in 280 ml of water until tender and fluffy.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Brush the wild salmon fillet with half the low-sodium tamari and bake at
      200°C for 10 to 12 minutes until just flaky, then break into coarse
      flakes.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Combine the hot freshly brewed sencha green tea, hot unsalted dashi, and
      remaining low-sodium tamari in a teapot.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Mound the warm brown rice into two deep bowls, arrange the flaked salmon,
      toasted nori strips, sliced scallions, grated ginger, and sesame seeds on
      top, and pour the hot tea broth gently down the side of each bowl.
