instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Whisk the sifted chickpea flour, water, 16 ml of olive oil, chopped
      rosemary, half the black pepper, and salt into a smooth crepe-like batter;
      rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Place a 28 cm ovenproof cast-iron skillet inside the oven and preheat to
      230°C for 10 minutes until thoroughly hot.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Carefully remove the hot skillet, swirl in the remaining 12 ml of olive
      oil, and immediately pour in the rested batter.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Bake for 15 to 18 minutes until set and blistered golden-brown at the
      edges, scatter with the remaining black pepper, and slice into warm
      wedges.
