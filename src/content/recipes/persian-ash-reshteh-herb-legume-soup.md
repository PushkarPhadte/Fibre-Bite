instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Heat 14 ml of olive oil in a large soup pot, sauté the sliced onion and
      garlic for 6 minutes until golden, then stir in the turmeric.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Add the rinsed brown lentils, unsalted vegetable broth, and salt; bring to
      a boil and simmer covered for 20 minutes until the lentils are tender.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Stir in the cooked chickpeas, kidney beans, chopped spinach, parsley,
      cilantro, and scallions, simmering 10 minutes until vibrant green.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Add the broken whole-wheat noodles and simmer 8 to 10 minutes more until
      the noodles are tender and naturally thicken the broth.
  - number: 5
    text: >-
      Warm the remaining 6 ml of olive oil with dried mint for 20 seconds off
      the heat, then ladle the soup into bowls topped with swirls of yogurt and
      mint oil.
