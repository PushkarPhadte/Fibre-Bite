instructions:

    text: >-
      Soak the mung bean glass noodles in warm water for 10 minutes until
      pliable, then snip into 15 cm lengths.
  - number: 2
    text: >-

      sliced Thai chili in a large mixing bowl.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Blanch the wood ear mushrooms in boiling water for 2 minutes, then add the
      shrimp and softened glass noodles for 90 seconds until the shrimp turn
      pink and noodles turn clear; drain well.
