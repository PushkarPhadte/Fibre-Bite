instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Heat 10 ml of mustard oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium heat and
      lightly sauté the bitter gourd batons for 4 minutes until browned at the
      edges, then remove and set aside.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Add the remaining 5 ml of mustard oil, temper the panch phoron and mustard
      seeds for 20 seconds, then stir in the grated ginger, raw plantain, sweet
      potato, drumstick pods, and eggplant.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Pour in 350 ml of water and salt, cover, and simmer over medium-low heat
      for 12 minutes until the vegetables are nearly tender.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Stir in the ground poppy seed paste, skim milk, and reserved bitter gourd,
      simmering uncovered on low heat for 6 minutes until the gravy turns
      lightly creamy.
