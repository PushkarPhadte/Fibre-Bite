instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 45 seconds, rinse under cold
      water, squeeze dry, and toss with 4 ml of toasted sesame oil.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Boil the sweet potato starch noodles for 6 to 7 minutes until tender and
      translucent, drain, rinse briefly, snip into shorter lengths with
      scissors, and toss with half the tamari and maple syrup.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Heat 6 ml of sesame oil in a wide nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
      and stir-fry the sliced onion, carrots, red bell pepper, shiitake
      mushrooms, and minced garlic for 5 minutes until crisp-tender.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Add the seasoned noodles, remaining tamari, maple syrup, and sesame oil to
      the skillet, tossing 2 minutes to heat through, then fold in the spinach
      and toasted sesame seeds off the heat.
