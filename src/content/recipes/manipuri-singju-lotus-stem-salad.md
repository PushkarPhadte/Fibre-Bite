instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Blanch the thinly sliced lotus stem in boiling water for 2 minutes, drain,
      and chill in cold water so it stays crisp; pat completely dry.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Dry-toast the chickpea flour and sesame seeds separately in a skillet over
      low heat until fragrant and nutty, then coarsely crush the sesame seeds.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Combine the shredded cabbage, crisp lotus stem, sliced red onion, minced
      ginger, green chili, and chopped cilantro in a large mixing bowl.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Sprinkle the toasted chickpea flour, crushed sesame seeds, salt, and fresh
      lime juice over the salad, toss vigorously by hand until evenly coated,
      and serve immediately.
