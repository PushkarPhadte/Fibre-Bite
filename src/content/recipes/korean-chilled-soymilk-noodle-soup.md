## Instructions
1. ठंडे, बिना मिठास वाले सोया दूध, फर्म टोफू, 12 ग्राम भुने तिल और नमक को तेज़ गति पर 1 मिनट ब्लेंड करें, जब तक मिश्रण झागदार और पूरी तरह चिकना न हो जाए। इस्तेमाल करने तक ठंडा रखें।

# Korean Kongguksu (Chilled Soymilk Buckwheat Noodle Soup)

Cooling summer Korean plant-protein bowl featuring 100% buckwheat soba noodles bathed in a creamy chilled soymilk and toasted sesame broth.

## Instructions
1. Blend the chilled unsweetened soymilk, firm tofu, 12 g of toasted sesame seeds, and salt on high speed for 1 minute until frothy and completely smooth; chill until ready.
2. Boil the 100% buckwheat noodles according to package directions, drain immediately, and rinse under cold running water until chilled and firm.
3. Divide the cold noodles between two deep bowls and ladle the creamy chilled soy broth over them.
4. Arrange the julienned cucumber and halved cherry tomatoes on top and sprinkle with the remaining toasted sesame seeds.
:::
