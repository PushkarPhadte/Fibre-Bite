instructions:
  - number: 1
    text: >-
      Warm 20 ml of extra-virgin olive oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium heat
      and sauté the sliced onion and garlic for 5 minutes until softened.
  - number: 2
    text: >-
      Stir in the trimmed green beans and quartered potatoes, tossing for 2
      minutes to coat with the aromatic oil.
  - number: 3
    text: >-
      Pour in the crushed ripe tomatoes, 120 ml of water, half the dill, and
      salt; bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and braise on low heat for 30 to 35
      minutes until the beans are silky-tender.
  - number: 4
    text: >-
      Uncover for the final 5 minutes to concentrate the tomato sauce, then
      drizzle with the remaining 10 ml of olive oil and scatter with fresh dill
      and parsley.
