# Georgian Lobio Kidney Beans with Walnuts

A Georgian-style kidney bean stew with ground walnuts, onion, garlic and herbs.

## Instructions
1. Cook the onion in the oil until golden, then add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
2. Add the beans, water and salt and simmer for 10 minutes, mashing some of the beans.
3. Stir in the walnuts, vinegar and half the coriander and simmer for 3 minutes.
4. Top with the remaining coriander.
:::
